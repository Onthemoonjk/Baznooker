BASNOOKER

Abstract:
Basnooker is a free and open source game that we three (not) merry men made in less than 30 hours or less (and even less than that). We tried to make it work, not work efficiently. Try not to hate it too much.

Tutorial:
There are only a few basic controls to know;
-> Firstly, wait for godot (YAAAAY, GODOT, WE LOVE YOU NON-FUNCTIONAL ENGINE!!!)
-> WASD for slow movement (not recommmended for actual colliding)
-> Left click to shoot a bullet, hold to charge bigger bullet
-> Right click to dash, hold to charge a bigger and faster dash
-> Hold E to slow down time (this is regarded as cowardly by us devs)
